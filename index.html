Does Eclipse work on older PCs?

Yes. Eclipse is optimized to reduce stutter, lower RAM use, and improve frame stability on lower-end hardware.

Absolutely. The HUD is drag-and-drop and modules can be enabled or disabled in seconds without fiddling with complex setup steps.

Yes. Eclipse supports 1.8.9, 1.20.1, 1.21.11, 26.2, and 26.3 to keep you ready for different setups.

Can I launch it with one click?

Yes. The client is designed for quick launch routines, with a cleaner setup flow and minimal configuration work.
